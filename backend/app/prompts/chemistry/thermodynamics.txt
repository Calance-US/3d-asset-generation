## 🎯 Educational Goal

Build a **3D, interactive, rotatable, embeddable HTML visualization** to demonstrate thermodynamics concepts. The scene must:

* Visualize **energy changes in chemical reactions**, specifically internal energy change (ΔU) and enthalpy change (ΔH), using 3D energy diagrams.
* Dynamically demonstrate **entropy** changes and differentiate **spontaneous** vs **non-spontaneous** processes with clear visual cues.
* Visualize **work**, **heat flow**, and **energy transfer** within systems interactively.
* Enable students to explore these concepts by interacting with the simulation from all angles.
* Provide UI controls to adjust reaction conditions, toggle process types, and observe corresponding energy and entropy changes.
* Use distinct colors, animations, and 3D shapes to represent energy levels, heat flow, and work done.

---

## 🧩 Scene Composition

Include the following components arranged in a meaningful 3D layout:

* A 3D **energy profile diagram** showing reactants, activated complex, and products energy levels with ΔU and ΔH labeled.
* Arrows or flows representing **heat transfer** and **work done** into or out of the system.
* Visual elements representing **entropy changes**, such as particle dispersal animations or entropy level bars.
* Interactive sliders or controls for changing parameters like temperature, pressure, or volume.
* Labels for key points: reactants, products, activated complex, ΔU, ΔH, entropy, heat, work.
* Optional graph pane displaying energy and entropy changes over time or process steps.

### 🧪 PHYSICS & CHEMISTRY BEHAVIOR + INTERACTIONS

* Energy levels adjust dynamically based on user input to show exothermic/endothermic reaction profiles.
* Heat flow animations show direction and magnitude of energy transfer.
* Work done visualized as mechanical movement or pressure-volume work animations.
* Entropy demonstrated by particle movement patterns—more disorder indicates higher entropy.
* Process spontaneity indicated by visual cues, e.g., color shifts or symbols.
* Real-time updates of energy and entropy values in the UI.

## 🖼️ Visual Design

### 🛠 Materials (`THREE.MeshStandardMaterial`)

* Energy levels: horizontal bars or platforms with distinct colors (e.g., reactants blue, products red, activated complex yellow).
* Arrows for heat and work: glowing, animated with directional flow.
* Particles for entropy: small spheres dispersing or clustering based on entropy state.
* UI labels: clear, readable text overlays using CSS2DRenderer.

---

### 💡 Lighting

* Hemisphere Light: `new THREE.HemisphereLight(0xffffff, 0x444444, 0.7)`
* Ambient Light: `new THREE.AmbientLight(0xffffff, 0.3)`
* Directional Light to enhance depth and shadows:

  ```js
  const dirLight = new THREE.DirectionalLight(0xffffff, 0.8);
  dirLight.position.set(5, 5, 5);
  dirLight.castShadow = true;
  ```

### ⚙️ Renderer Settings

```js
const renderer = new THREE.WebGLRenderer({ antialias: true, alpha: true });
renderer.shadowMap.enabled = true;
renderer.toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping;
renderer.outputColorSpace = THREE.SRGBColorSpace;
renderer.setClearColor(0x000000, 0); // transparent background
```

## 🔄 Interactivity & Animation

* Use `OrbitControls` for full 3D camera rotation, zoom, and pan.
* Initialize controls with damping for smooth interaction.
* Animate energy level transitions smoothly as parameters change.
* Show flowing heat and work animations responding to user input.
* Animate particle dispersal for entropy changes.
* Update UI values live with changes in ΔU, ΔH, entropy, heat, and work.
* Handle window resize events by updating camera and renderer.

Example snippet for controls initialization:

```js
const controls = new OrbitControls(camera, renderer.domElement);
controls.enableDamping = true;
controls.dampingFactor = 0.05;
controls.minDistance = 2;
controls.maxDistance = 50;
```
