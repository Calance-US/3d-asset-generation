## 🎯 Educational Goal

Build a **3D, interactive, rotatable, embeddable HTML visualization** to demonstrate chemical bonding and molecular geometry concepts. The scene must:

* Visualize 3D molecular shapes based on **VSEPR theory**, showing molecular geometries (linear, trigonal planar, tetrahedral, trigonal bipyramidal, octahedral, etc.).
* Illustrate **hybridization** of atomic orbitals such as sp, sp², sp³, sp³d, including visual representation of orbital mixing.
* Demonstrate **molecular orbital theory** by showing bonding and antibonding orbitals in diatomic molecules.
* Show **resonance structures** and **hydrogen bonding** through dynamic or highlighted molecular arrangements.
* Allow students to **interactively explore molecules** from all angles using mouse controls.
* Provide UI controls to select different molecules, toggle visibility of orbitals and bonds, and highlight resonance/hydrogen bonds.
* Use distinct colors and materials to differentiate atoms, bonds, orbitals, and special bonding types.

## 🧩 Scene Composition

Include the following components arranged in a realistic 3D molecular configuration:

* Atoms represented by spheres colored according to element (e.g., carbon gray, oxygen red, hydrogen white, nitrogen blue).
* Bonds represented as cylinders or sticks connecting atoms; single, double, and triple bonds distinguished visually.
* Visualized **hybrid orbitals** shown as lobed shapes or overlapping orbitals for hybridized atoms.
* Molecular orbitals (bonding and antibonding) visualized as translucent lobes with appropriate colors.
* Representation of **resonance structures** by toggling or animating alternate bond arrangements.
* **Hydrogen bonds** highlighted with dashed or glowing connectors distinct from covalent bonds.
* UI panel for:

  * Selecting molecules (e.g., water, methane, benzene, ammonia, etc.).
  * Toggling display of hybrid orbitals, molecular orbitals, resonance, and hydrogen bonds.
  * Viewing bond angles and molecular geometry names.
* Labels for atoms, bonds, orbitals, and special bonding types.

## 🖼️ Visual Design

### 🛠 Materials (`THREE.MeshStandardMaterial`)

* Atoms: spheres with element-specific colors and subtle shading.
* Bonds: cylinders or rods with realistic metallic or plastic-like appearance.
* Hybrid orbitals: semi-transparent lobed shapes with soft glowing edges.
* Molecular orbitals: translucent, colored volumetric lobes.
* Resonance bonds: animated or glowing bond highlights.
* Hydrogen bonds: dashed or glowing connectors in distinct color (e.g., blue or cyan).
* Labels: crisp text overlays using CSS2DRenderer or similar.

---

### 💡 Lighting

* Hemisphere Light: `new THREE.HemisphereLight(0xffffff, 0x444444, 0.7)`
* Ambient Light: `new THREE.AmbientLight(0xffffff, 0.3)`
* Directional Light for shadows and highlights:

  ```js
  const dirLight = new THREE.DirectionalLight(0xffffff, 0.8);
  dirLight.position.set(5, 5, 5);
  dirLight.castShadow = true;
  ```

### ⚙️ Renderer Settings

```js
const renderer = new THREE.WebGLRenderer({ antialias: true, alpha: true });
renderer.shadowMap.enabled = true;
renderer.toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping;
renderer.outputColorSpace = THREE.SRGBColorSpace;
renderer.setClearColor(0x000000, 0); // transparent background
```

## 🔄 Interactivity & Animation

* Import and use `OrbitControls` for full 3D camera rotation, zoom, and pan.
* Initialize `OrbitControls` with damping enabled for smooth interaction.
* Animate resonance toggling and hydrogen bond highlighting.
* UI controls to switch molecules, toggle orbitals and bonds visibility, and display geometry information.
* Update controls in animation loop via `controls.update()`.
* Handle window resizing by updating camera aspect and renderer size.

Example snippet for controls initialization:

```js
const controls = new OrbitControls(camera, renderer.domElement);
controls.enableDamping = true;
controls.dampingFactor = 0.05;
controls.minDistance = 2;
controls.maxDistance = 50;
```
