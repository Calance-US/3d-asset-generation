### ✅ EDUCATIONAL OBJECTIVE

Demonstrate that for a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, **pressure is inversely proportional to volume**. The simulation must help students intuitively understand how compressing gas (via a piston) increases pressure.

---

### 🧱 SCENE COMPOSITION (3D Setup)

- A **transparent cylindrical container** representing a sealed chamber.
- An **adjustable piston** fitted at the top of the cylinder.
- Several **gas particles** (as small floating spheres) inside the chamber.
- **Interactive slider or button** to compress/release the piston.
- A **digital pressure and volume display** panel updating in real-time.
- Optional: lab desk, ruler scale, temperature label (assumed constant).

---

### 🧪 PHYSICS BEHAVIOR + INTERACTIONS

- Gas particles bounce off walls and piston with elastic collisions.
- As the piston moves downward (reducing volume), gas particles speed up (optional), and **collisions per second** increase, simulating pressure rise.
- Show **dynamic pressure reading** (based on number of collisions per second or reduced volume).
- Keep **temperature constant** throughout.
